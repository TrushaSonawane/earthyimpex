Earthy Impex website — GoDaddy upload

This folder is ready for GoDaddy shared hosting. It contains the editable HTML, CSS and JavaScript source plus the logo, spice image, favicon and company-profile PDF.

Publish it:
1. Sign in to GoDaddy and open your hosting plan's cPanel File Manager.
2. Open public_html (the document root for your domain).
3. Upload this ZIP and extract it there, or extract it on your computer and upload the contents of this folder.
4. Confirm index.html is directly inside public_html and the assets folder is alongside it. Do not upload the enclosing folder itself.
5. Open your domain and check the contact links and page on desktop and mobile.

The inquiry form creates a message in the visitor's email app. It does not send or store inquiries on a server. A visitor must press Send in that email app.

The website uses the provided Earthy Impex logo and contact details. Confirm the full business address, product specifications, packaging, minimum order quantity, certifications and destination markets before adding those details to the site.
